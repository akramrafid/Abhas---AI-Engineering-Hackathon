# Plans, Pricing and Plan Changes

_Owner: LumaNet Fiber Product · Prices valid from 1 October 2026 · All prices in BDT per month incl. VAT_

| Plan | Download / upload speed | Monthly price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luma Basic | 20 Mbps / 20 Mbps | 800 BDT | Single device households |
| Luma Plus | 50 Mbps / 50 Mbps | 1,200 BDT | Most popular |
| Luma Pro | 100 Mbps / 100 Mbps | 1,800 BDT | Includes free Wi-Fi 6 router |
| Luma Ultra | 1 Gbps / 500 Mbps | 5,500 BDT | Includes static IP |

All plans are unlimited (no data cap). New connections pay a one-time installation fee of **1,000 BDT**
(waived for Luma Pro and Luma Ultra).

## Changing plans
- **Upgrades** take effect within 1 hour. The customer pays the prorated difference on the next bill.
- **Downgrades** take effect from the next billing cycle. A customer may downgrade at most **once every
  30 days**.
- Plan changes require identity verification (registered phone number + customer ID) and are performed by a
  human agent in the billing system.

## Static IP add-on
A static IP costs **500 BDT per month** on Luma Basic, Plus and Pro, and is included free with Luma Ultra.
