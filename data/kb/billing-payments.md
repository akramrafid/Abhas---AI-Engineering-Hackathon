# Billing, Payments and Refunds

_Owner: LumaNet Fiber Billing · Policy version 2.7_

## Billing cycle
- Bills are generated on the **1st of every month** and are due on the **10th**.
- A late fee of **100 BDT** is added if the bill is unpaid after the **15th**.
- Service is suspended if a bill remains unpaid **21 days** after the due date. Reconnection after payment
  takes up to 2 hours and costs nothing.

## Payment methods
bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Visa/Mastercard debit or credit card, and bank transfer. Payments made by bKash or Nagad
reflect within 15 minutes; bank transfers can take up to 2 business days.

## Double charges and billing errors
If a customer was charged twice for the same month, the agent collects the transaction IDs and opens a
"billing dispute" case. Billing errors are investigated within 3 business days.

## Refunds
- Refunds are only issued after a **billing supervisor approves** them. Support agents and AI tools can never
  approve a refund themselves and must not promise one.
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **7 to 10 business days**.
- Unused days are not refunded when a customer downgrades or cancels mid-cycle; they remain usable until the
  end of the cycle.

## VAT
All prices include 5% VAT. Customers can download VAT invoices from the LumaNet app under Billing > Invoices.
