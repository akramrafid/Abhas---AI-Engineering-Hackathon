# Account Security and Cancellation Policy

_Owner: LumaNet Fiber Trust & Safety and Customer Retention · Version 1.9_

## Account security
- LumaNet staff will **never** ask a customer for their password or one-time code (OTP), by phone, SMS, email
  or chat. Customers should never share them.
- After **5 failed login attempts** the account is locked for **30 minutes**.
- If a customer reports a suspected account takeover, SIM swap or unknown login, the agent must lock the
  account immediately and escalate to Trust & Safety. These cases are always handled by a human.
- A customer can reset their password from the LumaNet app using their registered phone number.

## Changing account details
Changing the registered phone number or the account holder's name requires a visit to a LumaNet service
centre with a national ID (NID).

## Cancellation
- Customers must give **30 days of notice** to cancel. Cancellation requests are handled by the Retention team
  (a human), who may offer a discount before processing the request.
- The ONT and router must be returned within **14 days** of cancellation. If the equipment is not returned, an
  equipment fee of **2,500 BDT** is charged.
- There is no cancellation fee after the first 6 months. Cancelling within the first 6 months costs 1,000 BDT.
