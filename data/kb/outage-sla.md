# Outage Credits and Service Level Agreement (SLA)

_Owner: LumaNet Fiber Network Operations · Policy version 3.2 · Applies to all home and SME fiber plans_

## Our uptime commitment
LumaNet Fiber guarantees 99.5% monthly uptime for every active connection. An **outage** is a continuous
period during which the customer's connection has no internet access because of a fault on LumaNet's network
(not the customer's own router, wiring or power).

## Outage credit table
Credits are based on the length of a single continuous outage, measured from the time the customer reports it
(or from the start of a confirmed area outage, whichever is earlier).

| Continuous outage | Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 4 hours | No credit |
| 4 to 12 hours | 1 day of credit |
| More than 12 and up to 24 hours | 3 days of credit |
| More than 24 and up to 48 hours | 5 days of credit |
| More than 48 hours | 10 days of credit |

One day of credit equals the monthly plan price divided by 30. Credits are applied to the next bill; they are
never paid out as cash.

## How to claim
- The customer must report the outage within **7 days** of service being restored.
- The support agent verifies the outage in the network monitoring system, then files an "SLA credit" request.
  Credits are applied on the next bill once verified. The agent does not promise a specific amount before
  verification.
- Planned maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance by SMS does not earn credit.

## Area outages
During a confirmed area outage the status page (status.lumanet.example) shows the affected areas and an
estimated time to restore (ETR). Agents should share the ETR and must not promise an exact restore time.
Outages affecting hospitals, emergency services or more than 500 customers are treated as **critical** and
escalated to the Network Operations Centre (NOC) immediately.
